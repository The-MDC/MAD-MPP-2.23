Status

What exists, and what does not yet

Exists today

  • The maths engine: conviction scoring, volatility, DL-LMSR pricing, the deterministic market classifier, the fee waterfall and card pricing, in code.
  • Binary and Progression markets, and the Z-Liar Meter, Gossip-Cop and Perpetual Epiphanies families.
  • A private-beta app with the MADHAT'r concierge, the Octavian live mesh, the card game and the open-markets floor on free-play $MADx.
  • RSS ingestion from public publishers, with a satire writer and a brand-safety gate that holds risky lines for a human.

Does not exist yet

  • A live pilot market with real creators and volume: the next milestone.
  • Market and Shield-vault contracts on a public testnet or mainnet. Resolution is owner-attested today.
  • A security audit.
  • Multiple choice, Over/Under, PvP and Chaos as app formats (designed), Market+ and P2P escrow (planned).
  • Active users beyond the waitlist.
