Certain-UnCertainty

Every existing perp prices one number — a velocity, a credibility, a persistence. This one prices a path. A what-if is a tree, positions ride a branch of it, and the tree never terminates — which is what makes it a perpetual rather than a very long binary.

Open conviction market Forward · what if this happens Counterfactual · what if this had happened
The live frontier DL-LMSR · Σp = 1 · leg roll 59.061d
You only ever trade the frontier — the sibling set at the live node. Everything behind it is settled history; everything ahead of it is unpriced.
On the name

One name for the whole system

Read as -ome — genome, biome, proteome, the complete set of a kind of thing — it names something on this platform that had no name before: the full tree of paths a market can take, of which any position holds one branch. Nothing else in the codebase covers it.

The official name is The Paseome: Certain-UnCertainty, set by the founder on 23 Oct 2026. It is the name on the card as well as the name of the system. The two sides inside it are described by what they do, forward and counterfactual. Foresight and hindsight remain only as internal labels in the code.

Certain-UnCertainty is the design in two words. The arithmetic is certain: a frontier's prices always sum to one, the market maker's worst-case loss is fixed in advance at b·ln n (1,318 $MADx for three branches, 1,931 for five), an untaken forward branch is refunded, and a leg rolls every 59.061 days. The outcome stays uncertain, and only in the kind of uncertainty that knowing more can reduce.

The two sides split exactly where the maths forces it, at what can and cannot be settled.

Layer 1 · Pricing

The frontier simplex

Why the tree can't leak value at a fork

The failure mode of a branching market is obvious once stated: if the prices on the branches leaving a node don't sum to the price of the node itself, the market either mints or destroys value every time it forks. Traders find that within a day.

The engine already contains the fix, in two halves that have never been joined. calculateDLLMSRPrice prices an n-outcome vector whose probabilities sum to one by construction. Feed that vector in as the conditionalProb edges of a TreeNode, and conservation stops being a rule anyone has to enforce.

P(branch i) = e^(q_i/b) / Σ e^(q_j/b)  ·  path = Π P(branch)

A position's value is the product of the branch prices along its path. Deepen the path and the price falls multiplicatively — long-shot what-ifs get cheap on their own, with no separate long-shot discount to tune. Hanson designed LMSR for exactly this: combinatorial markets over a tree of conditional outcomes.

Only the frontier is priced. Maintaining correct LMSR prices across a whole combinatorial outcome space is NP-hard, so the market never attempts it: it prices the sibling set at the live node and nothing else — O(n) per node instead of exponential. Depth is a property of the recorded path, not of the pricing problem.

When a leg rolls and a branch is taken, Zeitgeist's succession matrix already knows how to transmigrate conviction from a parent slate into its successor, under a write path that refuses a matrix that leaks holder value. That is the same operation, and it is already written.

Layer 2 · Settlement

One of these two markets can never be resolved

And the design is better for admitting it

The forward side is ordinary: the branch happens or it doesn't, the oracle says which, the leg rolls. The counterfactual side is not, and the temptation is to paper over it. A counterfactual has no outcome to settle against. Conditional market prices reveal probabilistic relationships, not causal ones — P(Y | X = x) is not what Y would have been had X been set to x, and no amount of liquidity closes that gap.

So the counterfactual side doesn't price the counterfactual itself. It prices the persistence of the belief about it — which is a real, measurable, continuously-settling quantity, and which is precisely what Perpetual Epiphanies already does when it prices Murphy's Law with no terminal date. The instrument is honest by construction: it never claims to know what would have happened, only how hard the culture is still holding the idea that something would have.

cgarchUpdate → { permanent, transitory }  ·  THE DIVERGENCE = τ_perm − τ_trans

Five Faces' component decomposition, pointed at a belief series instead of a mask. The permanent component is the part of the what-if that has entered the culture and stayed; the transitory component is meme churn that will be gone in a leg. The counterfactual side's headline print is the gap between them — the same reading Five Faces already renders, on a different input.

Grading comes free: brierScore and logScore are already in bayesian-conviction.ts. A counterfactual position is scored against the forecast series it moved, not against an outcome — so the leaderboard is calibration, which is the only fair thing to rank people on in a market that cannot resolve.

The one thing that will kill this if you skip it

The forward and counterfactual sides must be visually and structurally unmistakable from each other on every surface. A trader who thinks a counterfactual print is a probability of something happening has been misled by the product, not by the market. Different class, different accent, different verb on the card — and the counterfactual card should say “never resolves” where the others say a countdown.

Layer 3 · Supply

The caricature is the oracle's raw material

Where the shorts, storyboards and UGC actually plug in

The content isn't marketing around the market. It is the market's input. A branch cannot open without a rendered artefact — a short, a storyboard, or a UGC submission minted against a caricature card from the 992-deep deck. No artefact, no branch: that single rule turns creators into the mechanism that extends the tree.

M = t^1.35 · c^0.75 · d^1.45 · ψ^1.15 · (1+π^0.9) · m_meme · satire · liar · garch_amp

The meme multiplier is already in MADConv. An artefact's reach is not a new term anyone has to invent — it is the term that has been sitting in the conviction score since the beginning, finally with something real feeding it.

  • Creators earn on their own markets. A sliding 28.8% → 40% of the gross fee, by ladder rung. The rare 60% VIP profit share is limited to 4,888 seats. No new fee mechanism.
  • Resolution reuses Gossip-Cop's spec. Optimistic oracle with a $MADx bond and a challenge window, plus the community punchline vote already scoped in its §7.3.
  • Subjects stay opaque. subject_ref is never a person's name — that is already schema law in 0012_perp_markets.sql.
  • Caricatures are of archetypes, not people. The Five Faces set — Saint, White Rice, Megalomaniac, Narcissist, Sellout — is the cast. This is the guardrail that keeps a what-if engine from becoming a defamation engine, and it is how Five Faces already works.

Fixed 27 Aug 2026 · the tree no longer leaks

propagateTree used to sum conditionalProb across a node's children without checking that they sum to 1, so a node whose branches totalled 0.8 or 1.3 silently mispriced every position beneath it. treeConservationError now checks every node to within 10−6, and the tree route refuses a tree that fails with a 400. Sourcing the edges from DL-LMSR keeps it true by construction.

Decided

Three calls, made

  • Untaken branches are refunded, not written off. Decided 27 Aug 2026. People lock capital in a conditional market only if a bet that misses for an unrelated reason comes back to them, and a refunded branch is not a near-miss. The counterfactual side writes off nothing, because nothing there is ever unrealised. Lives in settleFrontier.
  • A frontier holds 3 to 5 branches. Decided 27 Aug 2026. Three keeps prices legible and liquidity deep; five matches the Five Faces simplex and the dial geometry. Past five the prices get too thin to mean anything. FRONTIER_MIN and FRONTIER_MAX throw on anything else.
  • Creators author on the open market. The platform authors less. Decided 23 Oct 2026. The Paseome is an open conviction market: creators at MinorCreator and above, or VIPs, author its trees and earn their creator share on the trees they open. The platform also offers trees of its own, to a lesser extent and for different uses, and those carry no creator cut. Lives in canAuthorBranch.

Still open

  • How is a VIP seat obtained? Bought, earned or granted, and does the CR8TR traction rung still qualify? The 4,888-seat limit is recorded in the engine but does not gate payouts until this is answered.
  • When does the artefact gate go live? From Phase 1 it keeps m_meme measuring something real. From Phase 4 the tree grows faster first.
  • What starts a counterfactual tree? An anniversary, a forward branch that failed, or free authorship.
Movement II

Why anyone would touch it

The instrument above is tradeable. That is not the same as wanted. What follows is five mechanisms — from reward neuroscience, humour theory and cultural transmission — that this particular instrument happens to sit on top of, and the exact place each one plugs into the maths. Then the market play they add up to, and the line none of it may cross.

The uncertainty ramp Fiorillo · Tobler · Schultz, Science 2003
The burst codes prediction error and rises with p. The ramp codes uncertainty and peaks at p = 0.5 — where a prediction market most needs a trader.
M1 The market's information frontier is also the dopamine peak Science 299:1898

Dopamine neurons carry two separable signals. A phasic burst codes reward prediction error and varies monotonically with probability. A second, slower response — unobserved before this paper — ramps until the moment of outcome and covaries with uncertainty, which is maximal at p = 0.5 and falls away toward both ends.

A prediction market wants traders in exactly the same place. At p ≈ 0.5 the information value of one more informed position is highest and the spread available to earn is widest. The arousal does not have to be manufactured — it only has to be found and surfaced.

Design
The Knife Edge. The deck's default sort is |p − 0.5| ascending — not volume, not recency, not heat. Every other venue sorts by volume, which points away from the information frontier.
Plugs into
computeEdge and shouldAct already exist. Knife Edge is the market-wide sort; computeEdge is the personal one. Two rankings, both real, neither invented.
The hinge
Peak-uncertainty ramping is also what a slot machine exploits. The whole difference is whether the uncertainty is epistemic — reducible by knowing more — or aleatory. A satirical what-if is almost purely epistemic. So: never put an aleatory term in price. Card packs may roll dice. The market may not.
M2 Information is a reward, even when it is useless Bromberg-Martin & Hikosaka, Neuron 2009 · Loewenstein 1994

The same midbrain dopamine neurons that signal an expected water reward also signal the expectation of advance information about it — correlated with how strongly the animal wants to know. This holds for non-instrumental information: an answer that cannot be acted on and cannot increase the reward. Animals pay for it anyway.

Which retires the idea that a market report is a nice-to-have. Loewenstein's information gap says curiosity is an aversive state opened by an incomplete answer; the dopamine work says closing it is itself rewarding.

Design
The sealed read. Every branch shows its satirical one-liner in full and its reasoning one tap away. The gap between the joke and the why is the information gap, deliberately sized.
Plugs into
The per-family mini report shipped in the deck already. Paseome inherits its shape — book, flow, maths underneath, insight — per branch instead of per family.
Structural
Zeigarnik: unfinished business is remembered better than finished. A perp never finishes — the frontier is always live. The instrument is structurally unresolved, which is a property, not a defect.
M3 A counterfactual is a violation that is benign by grammar McGraw & Warren, Psych Science 2010

Humour occurs when and only when three things hold at once: a situation is a violation, the situation is benign, and both appraisals happen simultaneously. A violation is made benign by an alternative norm, by weak commitment to the violated norm, or by psychological distance.

This is the most consequential finding for this product, because it explains precisely why the what-if framing survives where straight tabloid satire curdles:

A counterfactual is benign because it did not happen.

The counterfactual side gets McGraw's third condition free, from the verb tense. Gossip-Cop prices a claim about something that did happen — a violation whose benign-ness must be manufactured by tone, every time, by hand. The irrealis mood does that work structurally.

Design
The irrealis constraint. A counterfactual market's title must grammatically contain the counterfactual — had, would have, if. A title that asserts is rejected at write time. Schema constraint, not style guide.
Falls out free
Distance rises with abstraction and with remove from the real. So benignity scales with path depth: branch 1 is edgy, branch 4 is absurd and therefore safe. The satire safety gradient is the tree structure — nothing extra to build.
Enforced by
Opaque subject_ref, already schema law · caricatures of the Five Faces archetypes, never likenesses · the irrealis check above. Three independent gates, none of them tonal.
M4 Change exactly one thing Boyer · Barrett & Nyhof · Norenzayan & Atran

Concepts that violate exactly one ontological expectation are recalled and transmitted better than either fully intuitive or heavily counterintuitive ones. The optimum is a minority of counterintuitive elements against a majority of ordinary ones. Such violations are attention-arresting and inferentially rich without rupturing comprehension. The effect replicates cross-culturally — France, Nepal, Gabon.

So memetic fitness is not a vibe. It is a countable quantity with an optimum at one, and this platform already has a term waiting for it.

Design
The MCI score. Count violated expectations in a branch proposal. Reward 1; penalise 0 (that is just news) and ≥2 (that is noise).
Plugs into
m_meme in calculateConviction — currently a hand-waved multiplier. This gives it thirty years of cross-cultural evidence instead of a guess.
Craft rule
The whole discipline of a good what-if, in three words: change one thing. “What if Kenny stayed dead” is one violation. “What if Kenny stayed dead and was a lizard and it was 1840” is noise, and the score will say so.
M5 Gossip is the substrate, not the topic Dunbar, Grooming, Gossip and the Evolution of Language

Roughly two-thirds of free conversation time goes to social topics; no other single subject — politics, sport, religion — takes more than about a tenth. Language looks adapted for storytelling, and gossip is social grooming scaled past the point where grooming could work.

Design
The unit of distribution is a conversational unit — a thing one person says to another — not a chart. The satirical one-liner is that unit, and satirizeHeadline already produces it.
Sizing
The addressable attention is not “people who use prediction markets.” It is the two-thirds. That is the honest reason this family can be larger than the six that precede it.
The open market play

The meme is the order

Every one of those five mechanisms is a distribution mechanism. Put them on the frontier simplex and something unusual falls out: marketing and price discovery become the same action. A branch's artefact is minted with that branch's price embedded. Share it and the price travels with it. Someone acts on it and m_meme moves, which moves conviction, which moves price — and the moved price is what the next share carries.

Most venues pay for distribution and for liquidity out of two different budgets. Here they are one budget and one action, and the fee waterfall already pays it. Nothing new to invent on the fee side.

grossFee = volume × 1.88% · creatorCut = grossFee × rung · platformNet = grossFee − creatorCut · community = platformNet × 28.8% · protocol = platformNet − community

The rake is a flat 1.88%. A creator's cut comes off the gross, and only on trees that creator opened. The community's 28.8% is of what the platform keeps after that, never of the gross.

The Shield has a scale too. MAD Shield is opt-in loss mitigation, never insurance. A 2–4% premium moves recovery from 28.8% up to 40%, and the staking tier sets how far up that range a holder can go: Bronze 28.8%, Silver 32.8%, Gold 36.8%, MAD Tier 40%. Recovery tops out at 40%. The “open” is supply-side. The tree is public, so any eligible creator may author the next branch off any live node: a creator at MinorCreator or above, or a VIP. Market inventory grows without the platform writing a line of it, though the platform still offers some trees of its own for different uses. The frontier simplex prices each new branch against its siblings automatically. No operator sets a line, because DL-LMSR sets all of them at once.

Cold start is already solved. A new family has no book, so the forward side's seed frontier is generated from proposalsForToday — the scraper the six families already run on. The day's real headlines become the root; the first branch set is the what-ifs hanging off it. The market never opens empty. It opens at the news.

The line

One property of this design is genuinely dangerous

Near-misses are losses that recruit win-related circuitry in the ventral striatum. They increase physiological arousal, increase motivation to gamble, increase betting speed, and foster an illusion of control — and pathological gamblers show amplified striatal response to them specifically.

A branching market is a near-miss generator by construction

Every leg roll produces, for most holders, a loss that looks like it almost won. That is not an incidental UI risk that can be designed around later. It is intrinsic to any instrument where visible alternatives resolve against you, and it is the reason to settle these four questions before a line of it is built.

  • 01Refund, don't near-miss. Untaken forward-side siblings refund. I proposed this for capital efficiency; it turns out to be the harm answer too — a refunded branch is not a loss, so it cannot be a near-miss.
  • 02Never render “how close.” No “your branch missed by 3 points.” That one string is the near-miss, manufactured deliberately. It will be tempting because it tests well.
  • 03No fast lane. The measured near-miss effect is increased betting speed. calculateConviction already weights time held (t1.35) above capital (c0.75) — the ideology is anti-churn. Don't ship an affordance that fights it.
  • 04Keep the Turning. Six legs is one lunar year. A cadence in months rather than seconds is the strongest anti-compulsion property this platform has, and it is already canon.
Compelling

Epistemic uncertainty. Resolvable by knowing more — reading the source, knowing the subject, being funnier. Rewards attention with understanding.

Compulsive

Aleatory uncertainty and near-misses. Irreducible by any amount of thinking. Rewards attention with arousal and nothing else.

The two are separable, and the neuroscience says exactly where the seam runs. Everything above is built on the first column. That is the whole reason it is worth building.
