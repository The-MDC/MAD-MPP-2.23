## Governance Model

### Voting System
- **Quadratic voting** — maximum **24x** multiplier per wallet
- Prevents whale capture while preserving meritocratic weight
- Voting power proportional to $MADx holdings, capped at 24x

### Proposal System
- Tiered based on staking/holdings benchmarks
- Minimum holdings required to submit a proposal (amount set by community vote at launch)
- Community review → on-chain vote

### Transparency
- Third-party audits on: reserve spending, staking dynamics, liquidity thresholds
- All treasury movements on-chain
- Regular DAO reports (cadence by community vote)
