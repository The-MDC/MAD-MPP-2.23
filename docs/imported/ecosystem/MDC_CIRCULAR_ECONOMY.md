MADDAO, MADforge Labs, ∑n:tangled Marketplace+, and MAD Gambit are each standalone products. They are peers — none is a sub-product of another. Together they form The MDC circular economy.

## Circular Value Flows

**MAD Gambit** earns $MADx fees → MADDAO treasury. Users earn $MADx → spend on En:tangled assets. MADHAT's Gambit card NFTs tradeable on En:tangled.

**En:tangled Marketplace+** earns trading fees → MADDAO treasury. Quadratic Launchpad distributes MADforge-incubated projects. Casino (Golden Hands) profit-sharing feeds community pool.

**MADforge Labs** — MADdApp.MVP generates no-code dApps for founders. MoonPad launches community IDOs/NFT drops via En:tangled's distribution infrastructure. Innovation Center incubates projects with fractional DEV support and strategic mentorship. All incubated revenue flows back to MADDAO + community pool.

**MADDAO** treasury funded by all product fees. Governs fee structures, partnerships, and protocol upgrades for all products. Distributes grants to MADforge Labs. $MADx holders vote across the entire ecosystem.
