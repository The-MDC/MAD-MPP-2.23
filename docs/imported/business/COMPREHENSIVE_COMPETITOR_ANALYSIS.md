## CRITICAL FEATURE/PRODUCT SUGGESTIONS

Based on competitive advantages identified across all 11 platforms + 5 new entrants:

### 🔴 MUST-HAVE (Competitors Have, MAD Gambit Doesn't)

1. **CFTC Regulatory Pathway** — Kalshi ($22B), Polymarket ($9B), DraftKings, PredictIt, Robinhood ALL have or are pursuing CFTC licensing. MAD Gambit's "DeFi-native, entertainment classification" framing is a legal risk vector that the VC stress-test doc already flagged. **Recommendation:** Begin dialogue with CFTC counsel. The A16z patent assessment (89% patentability) could support a "novel financial instrument" classification.

2. **Mobile App** — Kalshi, DraftKings, Robinhood, Fanatics ALL have polished mobile apps. MAD Gambit roadmap has mobile in Q3 2026 but this is table stakes now. **Recommendation:** Accelerate mobile to Q2 2026 (alongside TGE), even as a progressive web app wrapper initially.

3. **Distribution Partnerships** — Kalshi is embedded in Robinhood, Coinbase, CNN, CNBC. Polymarket has X (Twitter) integration and ICE data feeds. **Recommendation:** Pursue Phantom Wallet, MetaMask Snaps, or Telegram Mini App integrations for crypto-native distribution. The "embed widget on gossip sites" idea from the VC stress-test is excellent — prioritize this.

### 🟡 SHOULD-HAVE (Competitive Differentiators to Maintain/Strengthen)

4. **Creator Revenue Share** — MAD Gambit's 40% creator share is UNIQUE across all 11 competitors. Zero other platforms offer this. This is the strongest growth mechanic per the VC stress-test. **Recommendation:** Make this the #1 talking point in all VC meetings. Build creator onboarding tools immediately.

5. **MAD Shield** — No competitor offers loss protection. Hedgehog's "no-loss markets" are the closest but work differently (DeFi yield, not insurance). **Recommendation:** The A16z patent assessment identifies this as the strongest patent angle. File provisional patent ASAP.

6. **NFT Card System** — No competitor has yield-bearing NFT game assets. This is entirely unique. **Recommendation:** Publish 10-20 sample cards publicly to build social proof and creator interest.

### 🟢 CONSIDER (Features From Competitors Worth Evaluating)

7. **Parlays/Multi-Leg Bets** — Kalshi's parlays generated $947.8M in volume. DraftKings' entire business model depends on parlays. **Recommendation:** Add parlay mechanics to the roadmap (Phase 3). The card-based UX could make "Scandal Parlay Packs" a unique product.

8. **Data-as-Product** — ICE is distributing Polymarket probability data to Wall Street. Kalshi has exclusive CNN/CNBC integrations. **Recommendation:** MAD Gambit's cultural event dataset could be uniquely valuable for media companies, entertainment studios, and marketing agencies. Build an API early.

9. **Futarchy Governance** — Zeitgeist pioneered using prediction markets for DAO governance decisions. **Recommendation:** The MADDAO could use prediction markets to inform treasury allocation decisions — self-referential and powerful.

10. **No-Loss Markets** — Hedgehog's model where funds earn DeFi yield while predictions settle is elegant. **Recommendation:** Could integrate with MAD Shield — users who opt for Shield could have their premiums earn yield during the prediction window.
