## Summary
The founding whitepaper for The MADdegens Community (MDC) - a community-led social project and CrowdfundDAO. Originally on Solana with 6,666 Megalomaniac NFTs, later migrated to CrossFi and eventually EVM (Arbitrum). Established the core mission of combating VC monopolization of Web3, building decentralized venture capital ("de-VC"), and creating an inclusive community for creators, builders, and innovators.

## Key Elements
- **Original NFT**: 6,666 Megalomaniacs satirical caricatures
- **DAO**: MADdegens Community Treasury + VentureCatalyst Lab
- **Mission**: Combat VC monopolization, fund grassroots Web3 projects
- **Team**: Nick McLeod (Founder), Sean Renshaw (Founder), James Turner (Community), Kai Link (CM), Luther B (Artist), Wes G (Artist)
- **Royalties**: 8% (45% Treasury, 35% Operations, 20% Artists)
