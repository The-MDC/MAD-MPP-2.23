══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
                     CARD STATS EXPLAINED
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════


Each card has THREE stats (1-100 scale):


    SCANDAL (Red icon: 🔥)
    ════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
    Measures: Controversy potential
    Affects: Market creation, headline triggers
    
    High Scandal = More volatile, more markets created
    Low Scandal = Stable, fewer dramatic events


    CHARISMA (Yellow icon: ⭐)
    ════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
    Measures: Social influence and following
    Affects: Faction bonuses, social proof
    
    High Charisma = Stronger faction influence
    Low Charisma = Weaker social effects


    CHAOS (Purple icon: 💀)
    ════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
    Measures: Unpredictability
    Affects: Duel outcomes, random events
    
    High Chaos = More unpredictable in duels
    Low Chaos = More consistent outcomes

                     EXPANSION CARDS
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════


CROSSOVER CARDS (784 Total)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Each character gets 7 crossover variants:
112 characters × 7 factions = 784 crossovers

Art Direction:
• Same character, different faction aesthetic
• Costume/setting changes to match faction
• Keep signature item but faction-ify it

Example: Will Smith Crossovers
• Senator Slap Smith (Political) - In suit, Capitol background
• CEO Slap Smith (Tech) - Hoodie, server room
• Champion Slap Smith (Sports) - Boxing ring
• DJ Slap Smith (Music) - Turntables, concert
• TikTok Slap Smith (Influencer) - Ring light, phone
• Trader Slap Smith (Wall Street) - Trading floor
• Sir Slap Smith (Royal) - Knight armor, palace



CHAOS FUSION CARDS (56 Total)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Two characters merged into one absurd entity

Art Direction:
• Split face (50/50 or merged)
• Combined signature items
• Hybrid costume
• Chaotic energy effects

Examples:
• TrumpYe - Trump/Kanye merge
• Elon DeSantis - Musk/DeSantis merge
• Kardashian Santos - Kim/George merge



LEGENDARY CROSSOVERS (32 Total)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Ultimate "What If?" versions (4 per faction)

Art Direction:
• Full transformation
• Maximum power effects
• Cosmic/divine backgrounds
• Complete costume redesign

Examples:
• President Will Smith - In Oval Office
• Rapper Trump - On stage with mic
• Tech CEO AOC - In boardroom



GOLDEN PANDERVERSE (8 Total)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Multiverse collapse cards (1 per faction)

Art Direction:
• Multiple versions of character visible
• Interdimensional portal effects
• Full gold metallic treatment
• Cosmic energy emanation
• Maximum visual impact

Examples:
• Golden Panderverse Trump - All Trumps merge
• Golden Panderverse Kanye - Ye transcends dimensions




══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
                     EVOLUTION VISUAL CHANGES
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════


As cards evolve, their art transforms:


STAGE 0 (Base)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• Standard art, clean design
• Neutral pose
• Basic background
• No special effects


STAGE 1 (Uncommon)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• Subtle glow added
• Refined details
• Enhanced shading
• Minor sparkle effects


STAGE 2 (Uncommon Rare)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• Particle effects
• Enhanced colors (15% saturation)
• Dynamic lighting
• Motion hints


STAGE 3 (Rare)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• Dynamic pose change
• Special background
• Energy aura
• Upgraded costume elements


STAGE 4 (Super Rare)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• Full costume redesign
• Animated elements (for digital)
• Power emanation effects
• Premium materials (gold accents)


STAGE 5 (Legendary)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• Complete transformation
• Cosmic/ethereal background
• Maximum special effects
• Godlike presence



EXAMPLE: WILL SMITH EVOLUTION
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Stage 0: Standard tuxedo, Oscar night pose
Stage 1: Golden glow around hand
Stage 2: Electricity sparks from palm
Stage 3: Slap motion blur, impact waves
Stage 4: Full gold suit, afterimage trails
Stage 5: Cosmic hand of justice, reality bending




══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
                     CONDITION VISUAL STATES
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════


Cards degrade through use. Show visually:


PRISTINE (100%)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• Perfect art
• Full color saturation
• Clean edges
• No damage marks


GOOD (90%)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• Slight wear on edges
• 98% saturation
• Minor scuffs


WORN (75%)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• Visible scratches
• 90% saturation
• Edge creases


DAMAGED (50%)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• Cracks appear
• 75% saturation
• Torn corners


BROKEN (25%)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• Major cracks
• 50% saturation
• Heavy wear


WRECKED (10%)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• Severe damage
• Nearly grayscale
• Barely holding together
