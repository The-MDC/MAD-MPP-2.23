BINARY MARKETS
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Simple Yes/No predictions

• "Will there be a royal family announcement?"

MULTI-OUTCOME MARKETS
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Multiple possible outcomes

Example Questions:
• "Which celebrity will be arrested next?"
  ○ Option A: Diddy
  ○ Option B: Tate
  ○ Option C: Other
  ○ Option D: None this month

• "What will Kanye's next controversy be about?"
  ○ Option A: Antisemitism
  ○ Option B: Politics
  ○ Option C: Fashion
  ○ Option D: Music beef



CONVICTION MARKETS
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Long-term sentiment betting

How it works:
1. Stake $MADx on an outcome
2. Conviction grows over time
3. First outcome to reach threshold wins

Formula: Conviction = Stake × (1 - 0.9^days) / 0.1

Example:
• "Will Elon still own X by end of 2026?"
• Stake longer = more conviction = more influence



SAGA MARKETS
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Season-long narrative predictions

Linked to 90-day Saga storylines:
• Multi-stage predictions
• Outcomes affect next stage
• Massive end-of-season payouts

HOW DUELS WORK
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Step 1: CHALLENGE
────────────────────
• Select opponent
• Choose wager amount
• Pick your card

Step 2: ACCEPT
────────────────────
• Opponent selects their card
• Both locked to same market
• Opposite predictions required

Step 3: PREDICT
────────────────────
• Same market question
• You bet YES, they bet NO
• Winner determined by outcome

Step 4: RESOLVE
────────────────────
• Winner takes pot
• Winner "steals" opponent's primary trait
• Stolen trait lasts 24 hours
