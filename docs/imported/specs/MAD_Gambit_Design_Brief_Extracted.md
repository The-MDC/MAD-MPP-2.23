01. Product Overview
MAD Gambit is a bold, irreverent prediction-conviction+ market platform experience built on Blockchain, DeFi, & GameFi rails. Unlike the 85%+ of prediction platforms that are copy-paste clones with minor visual variations, MAD Gambit is designed from the ground up to feel like a completely different category of product combining a serious liquidity engine with a cultural, meme-forward identity and a unique card game layer.

03. Screen-by-Screen Breakdown
Below is every screen/view I've mapped out based on your vision. For each one, there's a 'Nick's Notes' field please use it to add, remove, rename, or redirect anything.
Screen 1 : Landing Page (Public / Pre-Login)
The first impression for new users arriving at MAD Gambit. Sets the tone before they sign up.
Screen 2: Main Hub (Core Dashboard After Login)
The primary screen users land on after logging in. This is the heart of the platform.
Screen 3: Prediction Market Feed
Where users browse all active prediction markets and enter positions.
Screen 4 : Individual Market Detail Page
Deep-dive view for a single prediction market. Where users commit positions and track outcomes.
Screen 5 : Card Game Hub
A distinct section within MAD Gambit with its own visual identity — feels like entering a different room within the same building.
Screen 6 :User Portfolio & Profile
Personal command center user's positions, history, earnings, and identity.
Screen 7 — Onboarding Flow (New User)
First-run experience for new users. Critical for conversion and early retention.
