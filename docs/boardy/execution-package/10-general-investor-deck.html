02 · The problem

Prediction markets today settle facts once. Culture doesn't wait.

Polymarket and Kalshi are truth machines — they settle at resolution. DraftKings and Stake are liquidity machines — they move money on outcomes that already have odds. Neither prices the thing that actually moves fastest: culture, in real time, before anyone agrees on how it ends.

05 · The economics

Enforced in the contract, worked out on real numbers.

On a $1,000-volume market: 1.88% platform fee, always. A creator who's proven traction (34 markets, 888 followers) takes 60% of that fee — $11.28. A brand-new creator takes 28.8% — $5.41. Same fee, different split, gated by what they've actually built, not negotiated.

06 · MAD Shield

Losses aren't final. Loss mitigation — never insurance.

Opt in, pay a premium — live today, a 2–4% dial on your stake — and if the market goes against you, get back 28.8% of the loss at the standard tier, scaling to 40% shielded, 60% at our highest tier. This is a stop-loss, not a guarantee, and never described as insurance — that's a regulated term with licensing this product doesn't have.

Honest note on the mechanism

A more sophisticated, volatility-priced version of this formula is built and tested; the version actually live today is the simpler flat-percentage dial above. We'd rather tell you that than let a technical reviewer find the gap first.
