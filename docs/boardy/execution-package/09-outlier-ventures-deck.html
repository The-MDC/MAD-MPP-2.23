02 · The gap

Nobody prices culture continuously.

Polymarket and Kalshi settle facts once. DraftKings and Stake move liquidity. Culture — a headline, a claim, a story that either holds up or doesn't — moves faster than either can settle, and nothing prices it as it moves.

The structural point

Where "peer-to-peer" markets go one-sided, professional market makers step in and carry the risk — the same economic position a sportsbook takes, with a different regulator. Third-party analysis, not our claim

07 · What closing the gap requires

Here's the actual punch list — not a roadmap slide.

Security

MADShieldVault — the fund-custody contract — has no reentrancy guard yet. No contract has an external audit.

Settlement

The optimistic-oracle contract exists and is tested; it isn't called by the market factory yet. Resolution is owner-attested today.

Deployment

Zero contracts have ever been broadcast to a real network from this repo — everything so far is local/testnet development.

Consistency

A parallel, legacy contract package doesn't currently compile and is being formally retired rather than left ambiguous.

09 · Why an infrastructure fund

Reusable settlement infrastructure, not a single consumer app.

Continuous-pricing markets on claims that never formally resolve — DL-LMSR pricing, GARCH-modeled risk, a fee-routing and loss-mitigation primitive — is a layer any platform pricing subjective cultural claims will eventually need. We think it's worth building as infrastructure, not just as this product's backend. That's our case to make, not a claim about your fund's thesis.
