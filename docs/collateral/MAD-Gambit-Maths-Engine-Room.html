Where the chain runs

GARCH σ → conviction M → log-odds → propagateTree → VoI / edge → shouldAct → Kelly f* → position cap

Brier and log scores grade the realised outcome and feed the next prior. The tree is the only layer in the stack that can decide to do nothing.

The case for more of them

Deterministic gives an answer. Probabilistic gives a range. Trees give a decision — and a reason to stop.

Every other layer in this document is one of two shapes. Cobb-Douglas conviction, the fee split, the staking ladder and the quadratic vote are deterministic — same inputs, same output, no notion of doubt. GARCH, the Bayesian update and DL-LMSR are probabilistic — they carry uncertainty but never resolve it into an action. A decision tree is the third shape: it takes cost, information value and irreversibility as first-class inputs, and its output is a verb. Five places in the mastersheet are asking for one.

Oracle escalation

AI resolve → dispute window → human panel is currently a fixed cascade. Each rung has a real price and a real error rate, so the correct escalation depth is a VoI calculation per market, not a constant. A cheap market with an unambiguous outcome should never reach a panel; a contested one should skip the dispute window entirely.

Liar-meter interrogation order

The four signals carry static weights (0.35 / 0.30 / 0.20 / 0.15) and all four always run. Vocal-anomaly analysis is by far the most expensive. A tree ordered by VoI computes the cheapest discriminating signal first and stops as soon as the band is decided — same verdict, a fraction of the compute.

MAD Shield claim triage

Recovery runs on a 28.8% → 40% scale, capped by staking tier. An expected-cost tree over claim signals — position age, counterparty behaviour, market class — would pay out faster on the obvious claims and route only the ambiguous ones to review, without changing the tier promise.

market-classifier as a real CART

Progression / Saga / Z-Liar / Epiphanies / Gossip-Cop is already a rule cascade — structurally a hand-written tree with hand-chosen splits. Learn the splits instead, score them with the Brier and log scores already in the codebase, and the taxonomy becomes measurable rather than asserted.

Futarchy proposal gating

Conditional markets are expensive to bootstrap. Before spinning one up, a tree can ask whether the proposal's outcome is uncertain enough to be worth pricing at all — VoI on the market itself. Cheap proposals resolve at the vote; only genuinely contested ones earn a book.

Why this is safe to add

Trees compose with what exists rather than replacing it — they consume M and σ as inputs. Bounded depth means bounded gas, so on-chain cost stays predictable. And every split is a sentence a user can read, which matters for a platform whose whole pitch is legible odds. The honest caveat: trees overfit, so any learned splits need Brier monitoring in production, and depth 64 should be treated as a ceiling nobody approaches.

Synthesis · not in the mastersheet — the neurochemistry the constants already encode

Nobody set out to build a dopamine machine. The maths did it anyway.

Read the constants back through forty years of reward neuroscience and something uncomfortable emerges: the clamp band, the market-maker curve, the Fibonacci triggers and the Shield tiers are each, independently, a well-formed piece of behavioural engineering. Below, each mechanism is put next to the finding it happens to implement — and where the platform’s number sits relative to the literature.

Uncertainty is the reward

Peak firing sits at 50¢ — and so does peak entropy

Fiorillo, Tobler and Schultz (2003) found midbrain dopamine neurons fire in proportion to uncertainty, maximally at p = 0.5, not in proportion to reward size. Shannon entropy H(p) has its maximum at exactly the same point. So the DL-LMSR price is not just a probability — it is a legible readout of how much anticipation the market is generating. A book pinned at 5¢ is arithmetically boring.

5¢50¢ · 1 bit95¢

H(p) at the current market price: {{ entropyTxt }} bits — {{ entropyPctTxt }} of the available anticipation. Move the market-price slider in §4 and this follows it.

The clamp is an arousal governor

0.09 ≤ σ ≤ 0.68 is an inverted U with the tails cut off

Yerkes and Dodson (1908): engagement rises with arousal, then collapses. Below the σ floor a market is inert and nobody comes back; above the ceiling it is a casino and people leave frightened. The GARCH clamp was written as a risk control. It is also, precisely, the shaded region of the inverted U — the peak of the curve sits at σ ≈ 0.385, close to the midpoint of the band.

σ 0.02clamp band paleσ 0.90

Live σ sits at {{ sigmaTxt }} — {{ sigmaBandTxt }}.

Reward prediction error · Schultz 1997

δ = R − V. The market price is V — which makes MAD Shield a dopamine damper.

Dopamine encodes the gap between outcome and expectation, not the outcome. Because the book publishes V continuously, the felt intensity of every resolution is knowable in advance: a win at price p lands δ⁺ = 1 − p, a loss lands δ⁻ = −p, and the platform’s loss-aversion weight of 2.5 — slightly above Kahneman and Tversky’s λ ≈ 2.25 — means the loss is felt two and a half times harder. Shield recovery shrinks the loss arm. That is the honest trade: it protects capital and it flattens the affective range. Recovery at the 40% ceiling removes two-fifths of the sting, and with it two-fifths of the signal that makes the next bet feel like it matters.

Zeigarnik · the open loop

Three market shapes, three arousal profiles

Zeigarnik (1927): unfinished tasks stay resident in memory; finished ones are dropped. A binary market is a task that completes — one spike, then the position and the interest both vanish. A Saga re-opens the loop at every leg. A perp never closes it at all, which is exactly why Z-Liar rolls its legs rather than closing them, Gossip-Cop targets >30-day holds and Perpetual Epiphanies >45. The hold-time target is not a growth metric; it is the Zeigarnik effect written as a KPI.

The rest of the map — finding, mechanism, our number

Variable-ratio schedules · Skinner

Unpredictable reinforcement produces the highest, most persistent response rates of any schedule. VRS_TRIGGERS = [5, 8, 13, 21, 34] is a variable ratio with Fibonacci spacing, so the gaps grow but never become predictable in the way a fixed ratio does. The unlit numbers between triggers are doing as much work as the lit ones.

Littlewood’s Law is a schedule, not a joke

Perpetual Epiphanies lists it as a mental model; it is also the arithmetic of intermittent reinforcement. At roughly one perceptible event per waking second, a month delivers about {{ littlewoodTxt }} events — so a one-in-a-million miracle arrives monthly, unbidden. The catalog is a library of pre-built variable-ratio surfaces, which is a strange and rather beautiful thing to be able to say about a set of proverbs.

Moral outrage as a reward signal

The scandal-velocity weights are a hierarchy of social emotions: shame 1.8, anger 1.4, disgust 1.3, fear 1.2, sadness 1.0, joy −0.5. Crockett’s work on outrage-sharing predicts precisely this ordering — self-relevant shame above other-directed anger, and joy actively suppressive. Gossip-Cop’s satirical framing is the release valve: naming the market a roast converts moral arousal into play, which is the difference between a comedy product and a pillory.

Z-Liar as an epistemic-curiosity engine

Loewenstein’s information-gap theory: curiosity peaks when you know enough to feel a gap but not enough to close it. The LieScore bands are built to sit a user in that gap — SLIPPERY at 40–60 is the most engaging verdict on the meter, because TOTAL FABRICATION resolves the question and STRAIGHT SHOOTER dissolves it. The middle band is where the §4 tree says information is worth paying for. The same shape, arrived at twice from different directions.

Endowment, wear and the Affliction card

Endowment effect: owned objects are overvalued, and objects with a history more so. Wear accrual and the Affliction / Golden Aura evolution give a losing position a permanent artefact — which is the one mechanism on the platform that converts a loss into something a user chooses to keep. Note the ethical edge: this is also how a loss stops feeling like an exit.

The line this section will not cross

Everything above is a design account, not an endorsement. Two constants in the stack are load-bearing in the other direction: the σ ceiling of 0.68 caps arousal by fiat, and half-Kelly plus the δ-limit cap exposure below what a rational bettor would stake. Any future work here should move those numbers down, not up — and the §4 tree’s ability to return “do nothing” is the most valuable behavioural feature in the whole document.
